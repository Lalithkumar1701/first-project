01 · Our Story♡

How Navira Health Began

A Dream That Took Shape Over Time

Navira Health began with a simple question: “Why should people have to travel far for good healthcare?”

The idea of creating a healthcare centre had been developing for several years. What started as a personal dream gradually became a carefully planned venture with a larger purpose — to create a healthcare space that could serve people closer to their homes.

After years of professional experience in healthcare operations and understanding the challenges faced by patients, families and healthcare teams, Mr. Manikandan S. decided to transform that experience into something of his own.

The journey involved planning the concept, understanding the needs of the local community, building the healthcare team, establishing the clinic and pharmacy, and creating a healthcare environment where patients could feel comfortable seeking care.

On 16 July 2026, that vision became a reality with the opening of Navira Health Multispeciality Clinic at Karunguzhi.

From an idea to an institution.
From a clinic to a larger healthcare vision.
Navira Health is only the beginning.

The long-term vision is to build a trusted healthcare ecosystem that can grow with the needs of the community while maintaining the principles with which it began — accessibility, compassion, integrity and quality.

Better care, closer to home
