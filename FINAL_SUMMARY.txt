4. EXACT NETLIFY FORMS NOTIFICATION SETUP
================================================================================

⚠️  CRITICAL: EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS ARE NOT CONFIGURED IN CODE

You must manually configure in Netlify dashboard after deployment:

STEP-BY-STEP:

1. Deploy to Netlify
   • Go to netlify.com
   • Click "Add new site" → "Deploy manually"
   • Drag & drop the "frontend" folder
   • Site will be live in 2 minutes

2. Navigate to Forms
   • Netlify Dashboard → Your Site
   • Left sidebar → "Forms"
   • You should see "appointment" form listed

3. Add Email Notification
   • Click the "appointment" form
   • Click "Form notifications" or "Settings"
   • Click "Add notification" → "Email notification"
   • Set recipient: navirahealth26@gmail.com
   • Click "Save"

4. Test
   • Visit your live site
   • Submit test appointment form
   • Check clinic email inbox for Netlify notification
   • (Check spam folder if needed)

WHAT THE CLINIC RECEIVES:
   Email from Netlify containing:
   - name: [patient's full name]
   - phone: [patient's phone number]
   - email: [patient's email address]
   - date: [preferred appointment date]
   - service: [selected service]
   - message: [optional message from patient]

FORM FIELD NAMES (for email):
   name (required) - Patient's full name
   phone (required) - Patient's phone number
   email (required) - Patient's email address
   date (required) - Preferred appointment date
   service (required) - Selected service
   message (optional) - Additional message
